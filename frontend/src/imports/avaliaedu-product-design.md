MASTER PRODUCT DESIGN PROMPT

You are an expert product designer, UX architect, SaaS designer, and education technology specialist.

Your task is to design a complete high-fidelity SaaS product prototype for a web platform called:

AvaliaEdu – Plataforma Inteligente de Avaliação Educacional

This system is designed for Brazilian municipal education departments and schools to create exams, apply them in classrooms, scan answer sheets, automatically correct them using computer vision, and generate pedagogical analytics aligned with SAEB and SPAECE skills.

The prototype must look like a real production-ready SaaS platform similar to:

Notion
Airtable
PowerBI
Google Classroom
Stripe Dashboard

The result should be a complete product demo prototype with multiple connected screens and realistic data.

PRODUCT PURPOSE

The platform automates the full educational assessment cycle.

Workflow:

1 Login
2 Teacher generates exam based on skills
3 Exam is printed
4 Students complete the exam in classroom
5 Teacher scans answer sheets
6 System automatically corrects answers using OMR
7 Dashboard analyzes results by student, class, school and skill

The system replaces traditional optical reading machines with software-based OMR using computer vision.
